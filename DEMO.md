# 🧠 NerdQuiz

> **Gamified IT exam prep for ITPEC FE certification** — Myanmar's most popular entry-level IT exam.

---

## ❗ The Problem

ITPEC FE has a **pass rate under 30%** 📉. Students study from static PDFs and past exam papers with no way to track progress, identify weak areas, or practice under real exam conditions. There's no feedback loop — just papers and answers.

## 🚀 The Solution

NerdQuiz turns exam prep into an interactive learning experience. Students progress through a structured roadmap of topics, read concise lessons, test their understanding with instant-feedback quizzes, and build confidence with timed exam simulations — all while earning XP and maintaining streaks.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

```
  🗺️ Learning Map    →   📖 Lessons    →   📝 Quizzes    →   🌱 Mistake Garden
         ↓                        ↓              ↓                    ↓
   20 topics across         2-3 min reads   5 questions,      Wrong answers
   5 categories             teach the topic  instant feedback  collected for review
         ↓                        ↓              ↓                    ↓
    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                                    ↓
                          🎯 Weak Point Analysis
                         Topic-level accuracy,
                         color-coded mastery
                                    ↓
                          🏁 Exam Simulation
                         60 questions, timer, hearts
                                    ↓
                          📊 Admin Dashboard
                         Stats, charts, user mgmt
```

### 🗺️ Learning Map
A structured roadmap of **20 topics** across **5 categories**. Topics unlock as you progress.

### 📖 Lessons
Short, focused notes (**2-3 min reads**) that teach each topic before quizzing.

### 📝 Quizzes
**5-question** topic quizzes with instant feedback, explanations, and XP rewards.

### 🌱 Mistake Garden
Every wrong answer is collected for review. Students come back, see what they got wrong, and mark it as reviewed when ready.

### 🏁 Exam Simulation
Full **60-question** mock exam with a countdown timer and a ❤️❤️❤️ **3-heart lives system** — mirrors the real ITPEC FE experience.

### 🎯 Weak Point Analysis
Topic-level accuracy tracking with color-coded mastery 🔴 → 🟡 → 🟢 so students know exactly where to focus.

### 📊 Admin Dashboard
For founders: platform health at a glance — DAU, engagement charts, user management with search, filters, and CSV export.
